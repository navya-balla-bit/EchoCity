How urgent are the current reports?

Explainable scoring

Every priority score has a reason.

Priority = Severity + Impact + Waiting
Severity: 1–5 × 10 points
Community impact: up to 30 points
Waiting time: up to 20 points
Maximum score: 100

Scores help organize reports. They support human decision-making and do not replace on-site assessment.
